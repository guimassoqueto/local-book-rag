# Lifecycle and Measurement Checklist: The progress of library operations

**Purpose**: Test whether the requirements are complete, clear, consistent and measurable where the
feature is riskiest: the progress is threaded through the library's lifecycle operations and their
journal (constitution II, IV), it changes ingestion signatures (V), and it claims an estimate and
an overhead that only a measurement can show (VI). The display requirements are covered too, since
they drive what the operations must report.

**Feature**: [spec.md](../spec.md), with [plan.md](../plan.md), [research.md](../research.md),
[data-model.md](../data-model.md), [contracts/](../contracts/) and [quickstart.md](../quickstart.md)

## The lifecycle and the journal

- [x] CHK001 Does the plan say, for each operation, what a crash during each of its stages leaves
  and how recovery finishes or undoes it, as constitution II's check asks, or only that it is "as
  today, since the same calls are made in the same order"? Is that equivalence itself stated as a
  requirement that can be verified? [Completeness, Plan §Constitution Check II, Spec §FR-011]
  → research.md R15 gives the table, operation by operation and stage by stage. FR-011 now
  requires the same changes in the same order with and without progress, compared by the unit
  tests, and the recovery tests to pass unchanged. The plan's check II cites both.
- [x] CHK002 Do the requirements reconcile FR-011 and FR-012 with contracts/progress.md, where
  opening a stage key that wasn't declared raises `ValueError` into the operation? Inside
  `add_book`'s catch-all, that would turn a mistake in the progress into a FAILED book. [Conflict,
  Spec §FR-011, contracts/progress.md, research.md R10]
  → Nothing in the progress raises into an operation (FR-012): an undeclared key is logged as a
  warning and shown without a position, and a unit test fails on that warning for every
  operation. contracts/progress.md, data-model.md and R10 say so.
- [x] CHK003 Is the "Removing the earlier failed attempt" stage consistent with FR-015 and SC-009?
  In `add_book`, that removal runs in its own journal entry before `operation()` begins, so its
  stage line would carry no execution ID and its time would fall outside the operation's logged
  duration. [Conflict, Spec §Corrections during planning, §FR-015, §SC-009, data-model.md]
  → It is no longer a stage: it runs before the progress begins, like the checks, and takes
  moments (FR-007, the spec's corrections, R9, data-model.md). **[owner to confirm]**: the
  alternative, starting the addition's operation earlier to hold it, changes its log lines.
- [x] CHK004 Is it specified where each operation's progress begins relative to its journal entry,
  and not only relative to `operation()` and the lock? FR-017 says "from inside them", and R9 places
  the start after the checks. [Clarity, Spec §FR-017, research.md R9]
  → FR-017: the progress begins inside the lock, after the checks, and every stage that changes the
  library runs while the journal entry is open; R9 adds that the reindex's books stage opens after
  `begin_operation`.
- [x] CHK005 Is the failure outcome of a reindex specified to name the book and its step, and not
  only the stage "Indexing the books", when a book with a working version makes the whole reindex
  fail? [Gap, Spec §FR-004, §FR-008, contracts/library-tab.md]
  → FR-004 requires it; the outcome has `step` and `item` (data-model.md); the tab's and the
  commands' failure lines show them (contracts).
- [x] CHK006 Is the work that runs outside any stage named and bounded? For example, the catalog
  update after `ingest` in `add_book`, the undo after a failed stage, and setting a book to REMOVING.
  [Gap, data-model.md § The stages of each operation, Spec §SC-009]
  → data-model.md, "Between stages", names it, says the view keeps the last stage meanwhile, and
  that SC-009's second of tolerance covers it.
- [x] CHK007 Are the progress requirements for operations started by recovery, or by the benchmark
  runner without a `Progress`, stated explicitly? Today they appear only as defaults in
  contracts/progress.md: no stage lines for recovery, and stage lines for the benchmark's
  `add_book`. [Coverage, contracts/progress.md § Where it is threaded, Spec §Assumptions]
  → FR-015 says recovery logs no stage lines, and why; an assumption says an operation without a
  display shows nothing and logs its stages, as the benchmarks' will.
- [x] CHK008 Is the shutdown of the server during an operation specified as unchanged, now that the
  operation runs in a thread the handler starts rather than in Gradio's? [Coverage, Spec §Edge
  Cases, research.md R3]
  → The edge case now says the operation stops with the application, as today, since its thread
  doesn't keep the process alive, and recovery finishes or undoes it (R3, R15).

## Counts and the time left

- [x] CHK012 Can "a stalled batch must not throw it off for the rest of the stage" be verified
  without waiting for a stall on real hardware? Is there a deterministic criterion, such as one
  advance ten times slower than the rest? [Measurability, Spec §FR-006]
  → FR-006 now states it: with one advance ten times slower, every time left after it within half
  and double the truth. A unit test on a fake clock checks it (quickstart § 2).
- [x] CHK013 Is the behavior of the time left between advances specified in the spec, and not only
  in research.md? It counts down, and can reach zero during a long batch. [Gap, Spec §FR-006,
  research.md R6]
  → FR-006: it counts down between advances, never below zero, and reads "almost done" at zero.
- [x] CHK014 Are the rounding and units of the time left and of the elapsed time specified, such as
  seconds against minutes, and "about"? [Clarity, Spec §FR-002, contracts/library-tab.md]
  → FR-002: elapsed as 0:38 or 1:02:05; time left "about N s left" rounded up to a multiple of 5
  under a minute, "about N min left" beyond. **[owner to confirm]** the forms.
- [x] CHK015 Is the count specified for a stage whose total is zero, such as "Writing the summaries
  again" when every saved summary's chapter is gone, or a book whose extraction counts zero pages?
  [Edge Case, Gap, Spec §FR-008, §FR-010]
  → FR-010: "0 of 0", with a full bar; contracts/progress.md allows a total of 0.
- [x] CHK016 Is it specified that a count which would pass its total is capped, as
  contracts/progress.md does, and whether that is an error or silently hidden? [Clarity, Spec
  §FR-010, contracts/progress.md]
  → FR-010: held at the total and logged as a warning, never raised; the unit tests check the
  warning.
- [x] CHK017 Is the OCR switch consistent with FR-010 and SC-002? The page count is dropped once
  OCR takes over: does "every count reaches its total" apply to the pages read before the switch?
  [Consistency, Spec §FR-010, §SC-002, Edge Cases]
  → FR-010 and SC-002: a scanned PDF counts its pages to their total before OCR begins, then the
  stage drops its count and says OCR is running.

## Measurement and acceptance criteria

- [x] CHK023 Is SC-003's "at least 90% of the times left shown" defined per update at a fixed
  sampling rate, or per distinct value? The share depends on how often the area is sampled.
  [Measurability, Spec §SC-003, research.md R14]
  → SC-003: sampled every quarter second while a stage shows one, each sample counting once, over
  three additions of each book; R14 computes it so.
- [x] CHK024 Is SC-005 consistent with R14, which leaves the reindex that rewrites summaries out of
  the comparison? Is "today" defined as a baseline from `main`'s code, with its number of runs and
  the statistic compared? [Consistency, Spec §SC-005, research.md R14]
  → SC-005: medians of three runs against medians of three runs of `main`'s code, same books and
  machine, the reindex with summaries left out, as R14.
- [x] CHK025 Does SC-004 define "identical" for processed documents and passages, given that they
  carry the book's ID and revision, which depend on the order of operations in the workspace?
  [Measurability, Spec §SC-004]
  → SC-004: the same bytes in each book's extracted, normalized and passage files, both runs making
  the same operations in the same order on an empty workspace; R14 empties it before each run.
- [x] CHK026 Is SC-006's "exactly as the same failure does today" measurable without `main`'s
  code? Is it specified that the run without a `Progress` stands for "today", and which stages and
  operations "each" covers? [Measurability, Spec §SC-006, quickstart.md § 2]
  → SC-006: every stage of data-model.md's table, the failure forced through what the stage
  depends on, compared with the same failure without a `Progress`, which stands for today.
- [x] CHK027 Does SC-009 say whether the steps inside a reindex's books count toward the sum, or
  only the top-level stages? [Clarity, Spec §SC-009, data-model.md § The stage line in the log]
  → SC-009: a line for every stage, steps included; the sum is over the top-level stages.
- [x] CHK028 Does the measurement record everything constitution VI requires of a result? R14
  lists the commit, GPU, configuration and books' checksums, but not the models' digests and
  quantization, or the Ollama and package versions. [Gap, research.md R14, Constitution VI]
  → R14: the fields a benchmark run records (`Benchmark._describe_environment`), models with digest
  and quantization, Ollama and package versions included; the plan's check VI and quickstart § 1
  follow.
- [x] CHK029 Is SC-001's "within 1 second of the click" defined for operations whose checks can
  take longer, such as reading a large upload's checksum before the progress begins? [Clarity,
  Spec §SC-001, research.md R9]
  → SC-001: the operation shows within 1 s of the click, as starting while its checks run, and its
  first stage within 1 s of its progress beginning. R9 notes a 512 MB upload's checksum takes
  about a second.
